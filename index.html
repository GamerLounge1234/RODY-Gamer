A New Survival Experience

"Among Us: Lost in an Island" is a suspenseful horror-thriller series written and directed by RODY, where the line between reality and the world of Among Us suddenly shatters. Ordinary people from the real world find themselves trapped inside the game with no explanation, no escape, and no idea who—or what—brought them there. As fear spreads across the island, every task completed uncovers new mysteries, while every meeting reveals secrets that were never meant to be exposed. To survive, they must uncover the truth behind their imprisonment before the island claims them forever.

This series blends deep storytelling, psychological horror, intense suspense, dark comedy, immersive roleplay, and the authentic mechanics of the official Among Us experience. No mods. No special powers. Just pure paranoia, betrayal, and the constant fear that the next person you trust could be the reason you never make it home. Every decision matters, every clue has a cost, and every episode pulls you deeper into a nightmare where escape may be impossible.
